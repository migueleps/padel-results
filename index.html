    How the ELO is calculated

    Standard Elo, adapted for two-player teams and for the size of the win.

    Everyone starts at 1000. Results are replayed in the order they were played, one set at a time, so a set later in the day uses the ratings left by the sets before it.

    1. Team strength

    A team is rated as the average of its two players.

    2. Expected result

    The chance that team A beats team B follows from the rating gap. Equal teams give 0.5, and a team 100 points ahead is expected to win about 64% of the time.

    3. Margin of victory

    With as the difference in games, a win counts for more when it is wide. A 6–4 win has a multiplier of 1, a 6–2 win about 1.46 and a 6–0 win about 1.77.

    4. Rating change

    is 1 if team A wins and 0 if it loses, and sets how fast ratings move. Both players on team A gain and both players on team B lose the same amount, so the total rating in the group never changes.

    Beating a stronger team pays more than beating a weaker one, because is small and is large.
